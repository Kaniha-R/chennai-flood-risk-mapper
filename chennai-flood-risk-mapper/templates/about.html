ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Chennai Flood Intelligence, built for safer access.

FloodShield Chennai is an AI-powered urban flood intelligence platform designed to help citizens, emergency responders and local authorities understand flood risk, identify safer routes and make faster decisions during heavy rainfall.

OUR MISSION

Turn flood data into actionable decisions.

Instead of simply showing rainfall or flood locations, FloodShield combines environmental conditions, road risk, alerts and scenario analysis into a single decision-support platform. The goal is simple: help people understand where the risk is, how it may change, and which actions are safer.

What FloodShield Does

The platform provides a centralized view of Chennai's flood-access conditions and transforms complex information into easy-to-understand risk indicators.

  • Real-time flood and road risk monitoring
  • Interactive risk mapping
  • Safer route identification
  • Emergency flood alerts
  • Rainfall scenario simulation
